Temperature and humidity

By the end you will read a temperature and a humidity from one sensor.

2/7

The room can be warm and dry, or warm and damp. How do you measure both?

Temperature is one reading.

A fridge cares about both.

Humidity is the water in the air.

A DHT11 measures both, and sends them on one pin.

You ask the library. It gives you two numbers.

3/7

One pin, two numbers

Humidity

A small capacitor soaks up water from the air. As it does, its capacitance changes.

Temperature

A thermistor. Its resistance changes as it warms up. The sensor turns that into a number.

DHT22 measures a wider range more closely. It is used the same way, with a different type name.

5/7

Print both readings

#include <DHT.h>

#define DHTPIN 2

#define DHTTYPE DHT11

DHT dht(DHTPIN, DHTTYPE);

void setup() {

Serial.begin(9600);

dht.begin();

}

void loop() {

float t = dht.readTemperature();

float h = dht.readHumidity();

Serial.print("C: ");

Serial.println(t);

Serial.print("%: ");

Serial.println(h);

delay(2000);

}

float

Temperature and humidity need decimals, so the variables are float, not int.

readTemperature()

Asks the sensor for °C. readHumidity() asks for the percentage of water in the air.

delay(2000)

The DHT11 needs about 2 seconds between readings. Faster than that and the number fails.
