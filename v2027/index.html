Physical computing · Arduino · 15 lessons

Electronics that does something

Three units, five lessons each. Every lesson opens with one question and spends the hour answering it: a turn to a partner, one idea, a build, a test, a fault to find, and an exit ticket that answers the question out loud. They are written in order.

Year 7

Make it respond

On/off, one input, a rule, a number kept, a machine with states.

By the end of Year 7 a pupil can switch an output from code, read a button safely, combine two conditions, keep a count that survives, and design a short sequence of states.
